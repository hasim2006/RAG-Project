[Document 1]
Title: Policy 1 - Remote Work & Telecommuting Policy
Scope: Applies to all full-time and part-time employees working remotely.
Core Standards: Employees must maintain dedicated core availability hours between 09:00 and 15:00 local time. Remote staff are required to connect via company-issued VPN software when accessing internal servers.
Training Requirement: All remote workers must complete Security Module 101 by the end of Q3.
Compliance: Non-compliance will result in a review under Article 577 by HR and IT Security teams.
Contact: For technical setup questions, contact Division 4 IT Support.

[Document 2]
Title: Policy 2 - Information Security & Password Standards
Scope: Applies to all system users and network accounts.
Core Standards: Passwords must be at least 16 characters long and incorporate uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special symbols. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days. MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication) is mandatory on all logins.
Training Requirement: Security Awareness Module 2 must be completed within 30 days of onboarding.
Compliance: Violations may lead to immediate credential revocation under Article 203.
Contact: Contact the CISO Office or IT Service Desk at Extension 4402.
